FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What services does CRD Berlin offer?

CRD Berlin offers property management, apartment cleaning, concierge services and lifestyle management in Berlin. This includes coordinating repairs and tradespeople, communicating with tenants and owners, key handling, organising appointments and transport, and everyday personal assistance – all from one local contact.

Which areas do you cover?

We work throughout Berlin. Our office is in Moabit (Rathenower Str. 67, 10559 Berlin), close to Mitte, Tiergarten, Wedding and Charlottenburg.

Can you look after my apartment in Berlin while I live abroad?

Yes. We act as your local contact: regular inspections, key handling, coordinating repairs and tradespeople, cleaning before and after tenants, and communicating with tenants and building management on your behalf.

Do you offer cleaning for apartments in Berlin?

Yes. We organise regular home cleaning, deep cleaning and move-in / move-out cleaning for apartments in Berlin, and can combine it with key handover and property checks.

In which languages can I contact you?

You can contact us in German, English or Spanish.

How quickly do you reply, and how do I get an offer?

We are reachable 24/7 and usually reply within a few hours. Send us a message via WhatsApp, phone, email or the contact form and we will get back to you with a personalised offer.
